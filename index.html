The problem

A good event is easy to miss and a duplicate is easy to open twice. The same summit gets posted in a Telegram channel, summarised in a newsletter, and listed on the organiser's site — three cards, one event. A link that was live last week quietly dies. A price is never shown. EventOS exists to fix exactly that.

How it works

  1. ConnectTelegram channels, newsletters, Luma, Eventbrite, Discover NYC, Lantern, and organiser websites.
  2. IngestRaw posts stored verbatim, so nothing is ever lost and anything can be re-processed.
  3. ExtractGemini reads each post into one strict schema — date, venue, price, registration link — with a confidence score per field.
  4. ResolveDuplicates merged: same link, same title and date, same organiser and time. One card, every source kept.
  5. RankAn explainable score — topic match, what you saved before, timeliness, source trust — with the reason shown on the card.
  6. ServeA mobile-first feed: Home, Events, and My Calendar. Singapore first, past events archived.

What it does

Discover

Watches the sources you actually follow, Singapore-first, every day — plus hackathons.

Extract, never invent

If a venue or price isn't in the source, the field stays blank. A blank is honest; a guess wastes an evening.

Deduplicate

The same event posted three ways becomes one card, with all three sources listed.

Check the link

Every card can be re-checked: still live, sign-up closed, or removed by the organiser.

Track your way

Save, Interested, or Going — hold to confirm, so a stray tap never marks you as going.

Stay in budget

Extraction runs on Gemini's free tier, rate-limited by design: queued, capped daily, and paused before the limit — never a surprise bill.

Principles

  • Precision beats volume — the right twenty events, once each.
  • Provenance on every field — every card keeps its source links.
  • Never invent, always flag.
  • Fail visibly, per source — a broken source is shown, never silently emptied.
  • Source links go to the sign-up page, not to news articles about an event.
