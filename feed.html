Where these events come from

Add a source

Point EventOS at a whole platform

Paste a source link — a platform or directory like Eventbrite, Luma or Peatix, a venue’s events page, or an organiser’s listing page. Not a single event: a place that publishes events. EventOS checks it against the source guidelines first, then your Gemini key reads it and pulls its current events into your feed. The source stays watched below, and Refresh sources scans your watched sources again for new events.

Before a source is accepted: it must be readable without logging in, its link must lead to listings or sign-up (not news coverage), and a per-event page with no way to register does not count as existing. Login-walled platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram) and news round-ups are rejected, with the failed rule shown.

Your watched sources

These live in this browser, alongside your key. Refreshing this page keeps them; refreshing the sources below is what brings in new events.

How it works on this public page: there is no EventOS server scanning in the background. Mining and refreshing happen in your browser, using your key, when you tap Check & add or Refresh — that is the honest limit of a static page. Your private feed is the one with the automatic daily scan.
